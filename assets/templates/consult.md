# Consultation

## Who we are and the goal
Describe the project, people, current stage, and measurable outcome.

## Measured facts
Give observations, dates, units, sample sizes, and evidence. Separate facts from assumptions.

## What was tried and what happened
For each attempt: inputs, method, measured outcome, and remaining uncertainty.

## Constraints
List time, budget, hardware, skills, privacy, and decisions that cannot change.

## Numbered questions
1. What is the highest-impact next step given these facts and constraints?
2. Which claims need stronger evidence, and what would falsify them?
3. How would you apply your recommendation to our specific numbers?

## How to answer
Tag each claim [source] / [common practice] / [guess] with a confidence.
For [source], identify a checkable source; do not invent citations.
Give concrete numbers, ranges, units, assumptions, and tradeoffs.
No preamble or widgets. End with "what I would need to know".
